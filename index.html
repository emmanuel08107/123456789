Curriculum & Pathways

Comprehensive Learning Stages

From early childhood curiosity to senior secondary mastery, we provide tailored developmental stages for every learner.

Ages 1 - 5 Years

Early Years, Creche & Kindergarten

A nurturing, play-based Montessori-blended environment where toddlers explore, communicate, and lay strong foundational literacy.

  • Phonics, sensory perception, and motor skill enhancement
  • Caring, certified early childhood minders and safe play areas
  • Introduction to basic numeracy, rhymes, and creative crafts
Basic 1 to 6

Primary Basic Education

Fostering inquisitive minds with a solid grounding in English, Mathematics, Science, French, and Social Studies.

  • Computational thinking, reading mastery, and public speaking
  • Weekly computer laboratory hands-on training sessions
  • Continuous assessment and preparation for National Common Entrance
JSS 1 - JSS 3

Junior Secondary School

Transitioning into independent analytical thinkers with an extensive academic foundation spanning sciences, humanities, and pre-vocational disciplines.

  • Comprehensive preparation for Basic Education Certificate Examination (BECE)
  • Basic Technology, Agricultural Science, French, and Business Studies
  • Computer-Based Testing (CBT) practice in our networked lab
SSS 1 - SSS 3

Senior Secondary School

Specialized tracks in Science, Arts & Humanities, and Commercial Studies with proven record of distinction in WASSCE, NECO, and UTME/JAMB.

  • Pure Sciences (Physics, Chemistry, Biology, Further Maths, Technical Drawing)
  • Arts & Commercial (Literature, Government, Economics, Accounting, Commerce)
  • Career guidance, university placement support, and leadership development
Digital Skills & CBT

Computer Education & ICT Academy

Equipping students with modern computer literacy, coding fundamentals, typing fluency, and electronic examination skills.

  • Hands-on computer-based examinations (CBT) matching JAMB/WAEC digital standards
  • Introduction to Python programming, web development, and robotic systems
  • Safe internet research, graphics design, and cloud applications
